Frequently asked questions

How many books can I borrow?

You can make up to two borrowing transactions per calendar month. Returning a book early does not restore a borrowing slot.

When is a borrowed book due?

Each loan is due 30 days after the borrowing date. The Borrow page shows due dates and overdue status.

Does checkout charge real money?

No. BookNest is a front-end project. Checkout does not process payments or place real orders.

Where is my reading progress saved?

Progress is saved in your browser's localStorage on this device. It is not synced to a server.

Where does my feedback go?

Feedback submitted on the feedback page is saved in this browser only and is not sent to the BookNest team. To reach the team, use the email address or phone number above.
