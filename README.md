# 📚 BookNest

**BookNest** is a modern and user-friendly **online book library and bookstore interface** designed to bring reading, borrowing, and buying books together in one place.

The project provides a clean dashboard-style layout where users can explore trending books, browse different categories, manage their cart, and keep track of their currently reading books.

---

## ✨ Features

* 🏠 **Home Dashboard** — A centralized dashboard for accessing different sections.
* 🔎 **Book Search** — Search books by title, author, or category.
* 📖 **Read Books** — Access digital books instantly online.
* 📚 **Borrow Books** — Borrow physical books from the library.
* 🛒 **Buy Books** — Browse and purchase favourite books.
* 🔥 **Trending Books** — Displays popular books with ratings and prices.
* 🗂️ **Book Categories** — Explore books by interests such as:

  * Fiction
  * Programming
  * Data Science
  * Self Help
  * Business
  * History
* 🛍️ **Shopping Cart** — View selected books, prices, and total cost.
* 📊 **Reading Progress** — Track progress of the currently reading book.
* 🎯 **Reading Challenge** — Track monthly reading goals.
* 👤 **User Dashboard** — Includes profile, library, settings, and support sections.
